Greetings!

Hello! My name is Gabriel.

I am currently in my fifth year living in Japan and pursuing a Master's degree at the Graduate School of Engineering, Nagoya University, while working as a freelance English instructor. I offer comprehensive support ranging from everyday conversation and native-style speaking to standardized test preparation (TOEIC / TOEFL / IELTS / Eiken) and foundational English grammar review.

Whether you want to enjoy casual conversation or prepare seriously for studying abroad, students of all goals and proficiency levels are welcome!

At university, I served as the leader of an international student organization, planning events and communicating with people from diverse cultural backgrounds. My lessons have a relaxed atmosphere and are tailored to each student's individual pace. Please feel free to reach out anytime; I look forward to meeting you!
